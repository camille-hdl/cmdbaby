Privacy

What you type

CmdBaby never records, stores or sends a keystroke. It uses the Accessibility permission only to block shortcuts during a session.

Visit counts

The app has no account, no usage statistics and no advertising.

This website counts visits with GoatCounter, without cookies. It records the page, the referring site, the browser, the screen size and the country. Your IP address is used to tell visits apart, then discarded. Cloudflare, which serves the site, keeps standard technical logs.

Network

The app goes online only to check for updates. It reads https://cmdbaby.app/appcast.xml from time to time. Each check sends your IP address and a User-Agent header with the CmdBaby and Sparkle versions, and nothing else about your Mac. You can turn the check off in Settings, General.

Updates download from GitHub, where GitHub's privacy policy applies.

Your settings

Settings stay on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/CmdBaby/ and in the app's preferences.
