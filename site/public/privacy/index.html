Privacy

Keystrokes

No keystroke is recorded, stored or sent. The Accessibility permission is only used to filter shortcuts during a session.

No tracking

No account, no usage statistics, no advertising. This website has no cookies and no analytics. Cloudflare, which serves it, keeps standard technical logs.

Network

The app connects to the network only to check for updates (Sparkle). It reads https://cmdbaby.app/appcast.xml regularly; you can turn this check off in Settings › General. Each check sends your IP address and a User-Agent header with the versions of CmdBaby and Sparkle. No system profile is sent.

An update is downloaded from github.com, where GitHub’s privacy policy applies.

Settings

Settings stay on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/CmdBaby/ and in the app’s preferences.
