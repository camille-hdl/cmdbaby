Your toddler wants to type like you.

CmdBaby lets them. It covers the screen and turns off the shortcuts, so small hands can bang on the keyboard and watch things happen, while your work stays untouched behind it.

Why CmdBaby

You work from home. Your little one climbs onto your lap and wants to type on the keyboard, like you. Babies copy their parents all the time.

An unplugged keyboard does not fool them for long: nothing happens on screen. With CmdBaby, every key makes something appear. They type like you, your work stays safe, and after a few minutes they move on to something else.

The screen answers back

Pick a calm scene: fish swimming by, a little starship, or green characters raining down the screen. (Your toddler won't get that reference, but parents can have fun too.)

Your work stays where you left it

During a session, CmdBaby covers every screen of the Mac and turns off the shortcuts that quit apps, switch windows or open Spotlight.

It needs the Accessibility permission. It never records what is typed.

⌘ Q ⌘ Tab ⌘ Space ⌃ → ⇧⌘ 4 F11 Globe

Getting back to work

When it is time, type parent and Return, hold Shift-Escape for 1.5 seconds, or click the small square in the corner 5 times.

A timer can also end the session by itself, after 3 minutes by default. Choose the exits, the phrase and the duration in Settings.

Free and private

No account, no ads, no usage statistics. The app only goes online to check for updates. Read the privacy page.
