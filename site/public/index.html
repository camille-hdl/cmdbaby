CmdBaby

Let your toddler bash the keyboard. Your Mac stays safe.

Download for macOS

Free · macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon & Intel

Or with Homebrew: brew install --cask camille-hdl/tap/cmdbaby

Full-screen scenes

An undersea world with fish and bubbles, a green terminal rain, or a starship that fires at the key just pressed. Every key does something.

Shortcuts blocked

During a session, system shortcuts are swallowed so the Mac stays safe. This needs the Accessibility permission. No keystroke is ever recorded.

Exits for grown-ups

Type the phrase parent then Return, hold Shift-Escape for 1.5 seconds, click the corner square 5 times, or let the timer end the session (3 minutes by default).
