Support

Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub, or write to support@cmdbaby.app. Please include your version of CmdBaby (Settings › About) and of macOS.

Questions

How do I end a session?

Type the phrase parent then Return, hold Shift-Escape for 1.5 seconds, click the corner square 5 times, or wait for the timer. Each exit can be turned on or off in Settings › Exits.

Shortcuts are not blocked.

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and turn on CmdBaby. If it is already on, remove it with “–”, then add it again.

Why must the app be in Applications?

The Accessibility permission is tied to the app’s location. Move CmdBaby to the Applications folder, then open it from there.
