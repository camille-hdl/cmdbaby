Support

Report a problem

Write to support@cmdbaby.app. To report a security vulnerability privately, see the project's security page. Please include your CmdBaby version (Settings, About) and your macOS version.

Common questions

How do I end a session?

Type parent, then Return. Or hold Shift-Escape for 1.5 seconds, click the small square in the corner 5 times, or wait for the timer. You can turn each exit on or off in Settings, Exits.

Shortcuts still work during a session

Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, Accessibility, and turn on CmdBaby. If it is already on, remove it with the minus button and add it again.

Why does the app have to be in Applications?

macOS ties the Accessibility permission to where the app lives. Move CmdBaby to the Applications folder and open it from there.
